# What is Constitutional AI?

Constitutional AI is Anthropic's approach to AI safety that uses human-defined principles (the "constitution") to:

1. **Guide AI behavior** - The constitution defines what behaviors are helpful, harmless, and honest
2. **Self-critique and revision** - Claude can evaluate its own responses against constitutional principles
3. **Scalable oversight** - Reduces need for constant human feedback by encoding values upfront

# Relevance to Legal Practice

Understanding Claude's constitution is relevant for lawyers using AI tools because:

- **Transparency**: Know what values guide the system's responses
- **Limitations**: Understand built-in constraints that might affect legal analysis
- **Verification**: Recognize that even well-intentioned AI guardrails don't replace human judgment
- **Ethics**: Consider how AI system values align (or don't) with professional responsibility obligations

The constitution explains *why* Claude responds the way it does to certain queries, which helps users work more effectively within those constraints or recognize when a different tool might be needed.
